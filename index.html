The story

Nine of the top ten trained for the interview board at V&R

Vajiram & Ravi · New Delhi

The final result of the UPSC Civil Services Examination 2025 places all ten of the top All India Ranks with aspirants who prepared at Vajiram & Ravi.

Anuj Agnihotri heads the list at AIR 1, having prepared for the Personality Test with the institute’s Interview Guidance Programme (IGP).

9 of the ten went through IGP before facing the board. Akansh Dhull, AIR 3, came through the classroom GS Prelims cum Mains programme.

Their programmes ranged from the PowerUp Prelims and Mains test series to Sociology optional coaching and the GS Ethics module.

In brief

संक्षेप में

  1. 01

    One exam, three stages

    The UPSC Civil Services Examination is taken in three stages: the objective Prelims, the written Mains and the Interview, the Personality Test. One preparation serves the IAS, IPS, IFS and every other Civil Service.

  2. 02

    Classes in Hindi as well

    Vajiram & Ravi teaches in English and Hindi, with Hindi-medium mentorship and a Mains Test Series in Hindi.

  3. 03

    Two centres, and online

    Classrooms in New Delhi, on Pusa Road in Old Rajinder Nagar, and in Chennai. Live online and recorded classes carry the same faculty and plan to aspirants anywhere.

  4. 04

    Optional subjects covered

    Optional coaching runs for 20 to 30 weeks in subjects including Geography, History, Sociology, Psychology, Economics and Political Science, with an Optional Test Series alongside.
