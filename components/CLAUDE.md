## The Programmes section (#courses) is different: TypeScript + Tailwind

Ported from the soloboy branch (user 2026-11-13). One client component, `components/sections/Programmes.tsx` (user 2026-11-24: the section, shelf, cover art, TextMotion, IndianLink and Medallion in one file, `cu:` Tailwind utilities, no stylesheet of its own). Its programme data and links are `lib/data/programmes.ts` (server code, the course page and the hero, reads it, so it cannot live in the client file). Images in `public/assets/img/programmes/`.
- Its Tailwind setup (soloboy's colours, shadows, the `#courses`-scoped reset, keyframes) is in the shared entry `app/tailwind-sections.css`; its plain-CSS hooks (`peel`, `is-peeling`, `prog__*`, `tm-mask`) are class names the code or other sheets look up, not styles.
- Its section title, eyebrow and body copy use this site's styles (`SectionLabel`, `.h2.h2--sec`); its ground is this site's (`--ground-jaali`).
- Cards on the shelf (user 2026-11-25, "make each book just a card"): each cover keeps its design (gold frame, title, emblem, category)
  but has no spine or page edges (the gold frame and the divider are back, user 2026-11-25; the title in Title Case, `titleCase`), square corners (no radius), the same 195x230 size and the wooden shelf; the shelf, filters (no count badges since user 2026-11-25), deal motion and open pages are unchanged.
  Shelf physics after shopify.com/editions (user 2026-11-25): at rest each card leans back against the wall (`REST_TILT`, rotateX 10deg from its foot; when the pointer leaves it falls back with gravity and bounces once off the wall, `FALL`, a `linear()` easing, user 2026-11-25, a
  soft `REST_SHADOW`); on hover it springs up 26px and straightens (`HOVER_TILT`, `SPRING` easing, a larger `HOVER_SHADOW`), follows the
  pointer a little, and "View course" shows near its foot (`FOLLOW_BOTTOM` 18px). Clicking a card goes to its course page (user 2026-11-25: the book no longer opens; the open/page-turn code is kept, unused).
- Responsive: `max-[640px]:` / `max-[900px]:` utilities in the component and the shelf's geometry in `CourseShelf` (4 / 3 / 2 books to a plank, the stage scaled to fit); phones get the book rail instead of the shelf.
- Its motion is soloboy's (`TextMotion`, Web Animations: the site-motion check cannot see it and lists those elements as static).
